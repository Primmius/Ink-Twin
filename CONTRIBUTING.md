# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Ink-Twin. 

If you submit a pull request, you must confirm that:
1. The submission is entirely your own original work.
2. Your contribution is lawful and does not infringe on any third-party rights.
3. You have full legal permission for any datasets, model prompts, or assets you include.
4. The submission is compatible with the terms set out in `LICENSE.txt` and does not attempt to subvert the protections on brand assets, demo content, or evaluation data.
